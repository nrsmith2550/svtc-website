Biography

Coach Shane Valko and Foundations of Silent Victory

Shane Valko has built his life around excellence in sport and fitness. Years of high-level competition and a deep toolbox of modern training methods have made him a coach that athletes of every age seek out.

It started with wrestling, a sport that tests the body and the mind in equal measure, and Shane's work ethic carried him to the top of it. He's an NCAA Hall of Fame wrestler and a three-time All-American (1st in 2010, 2nd in 2009, 4th in 2008), after qualifying for nationals in 2007. His 2010 season brought the NCAA National Championship, NCAA National Tournament Outstanding Wrestler, East Coast Wrestler of the Year and National Wrestler of the Year. He played collegiate golf as well.

When his competitive days wound down, Shane turned to coaching so he could hand what he'd learned to the next generation. The gym he founded quickly became a home for specialized athletic training, with programs covering advanced strength and conditioning, sport-specific work and injury prevention, each shaped around what an athlete actually needs.

Shane also competed in professional MMA, and as a professional Jiu-Jitsu brown belt he remains undefeated at 7 wins, 0 losses and 1 draw.

The coaching résumé matches the competitive one: 38 All-Americans and 4 National Champions developed under his guidance.

Under one roof, athletes have a Jiu-Jitsu studio, wrestling programs and an on-site chiropractic clinic for individualized performance and recovery care. Bringing these disciplines together is central to how Shane builds complete, adaptable athletes.

His core belief is simple: with the right guidance, support and training, any athlete can do great things. He's built a gym where people of all ages are pushed to their limits and helped to their personal bests, and that steady commitment has earned him a loyal following and a name as a mentor the athletic community trusts.

Outside the gym, Shane keeps competing with himself across a range of sports, always hunting for the next challenge and a new way to inspire the people around him. That energy, and his dedication to other people's goals, is what makes him a leader in specialized athletic training.
