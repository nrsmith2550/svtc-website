Welcome to Silent Victory Training Center

Shane Valko opened Silent Victory in 2013 with one goal: build a room where anyone, from first-timers to seasoned competitors, could get better. Since then it has become a go-to destination for athletes chasing their potential, a community-driven gym that values growth, dedication and excellence, with world-class coaching and top-tier resources for every level.

Everything here reflects Shane's standards. He brings years of experience across strength training, wrestling and Jiu-Jitsu, plus a real drive to see people hit their goals. Members sharpen their skills, test their limits and see results they can measure, and his hands-on style means nobody gets lost in the crowd. You'll train in a space that's safe, focused and motivating.

The athletes who walk through our doors tend to stay. They share the same hunger to improve, and the culture runs on discipline, mental toughness and teamwork. Hard work gets recognized here, and wins of every size belong to the whole team. Individual coaching, a welcoming atmosphere and a focus on developing the complete athlete are why Silent Victory continues to stand out.

Our Mission
Why We Train

Our Mission

We exist to give athletes a world-class place to train, one that helps them become their best on the mat and in life. That means individualized coaching in wrestling, Jiu-Jitsu and strength training from instructors who've succeeded at the highest levels, and a program that builds technique, mental toughness and conditioning together.

Respect, discipline and hard work are the foundation of our community, and everyone is welcome in it. We want every member to leave more confident than they came in, and we celebrate progress whether it's a state title or a first takedown. Each obstacle is a chance to get stronger. Victory is earned quietly, through the work you put in when no one is watching.
