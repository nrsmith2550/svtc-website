Manage Site Photos

Sign in to change any photo on the website. Every picture is listed by page and section, for example Home page → Top banner.

  1. Click Owner Login below and sign in with the email you were invited with (or your GitHub account).
  2. Open svtc-website, then Website Photos.
  3. Find the photo you want to change, click it, and upload a new picture.
  4. Click Save. The live site updates in about a minute.
